A marketplace with a heart.

MEEM brings Muslim-friendly brands together so you can shop with confidence. And every time you do, someone in need is helped.

Trusted brands

Every shop is faith-aligned and checked, so you know what you are buying and who made it.

Muslim entrepreneurs

How it works

Four simple steps.

From a seller's first listing to a donation reaching someone in need.

01

Sellers join

Muslim entrepreneurs open a shop on MEEM and list their products. Setup is simple.

02

Buyers shop

Shoppers browse the app or website and buy from brands they can trust.

03

A donation is set aside

With every order, MEEM automatically sets aside a small donation. No extra clicks.

04

Everything is tracked

MEEM runs the platform, supports sellers and records every donation openly.
